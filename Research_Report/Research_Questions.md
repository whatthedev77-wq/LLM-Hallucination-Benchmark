# Research Questions
## Main Research Question
How do different LLM's differ in thier hallucination rates across different domains like : Maths,
Programing, general knowledge etc under same conditions?
## Research Objectives
1. Compare hallucination rates of selected LLM  Models.
2. Compare hallucination rates in different domains.
3. Investigate the effect of difficulty on hallucination.
4. Analyze the relationship between hallucination, accuracy, and refusal behavior.
## Hypotheses
### H1 
Hallucination rates will differ with different LLM's model based on their parameters.
### H2
Hallucination rates will differ with different domains based on the diffculty of the domain 
### H3
More the difficult question the right is the answer it will be easier for the model to give answer to a difficult question for a human.
### H4
Models with greater refusal behavior may produce fewer hallucinated answers.
