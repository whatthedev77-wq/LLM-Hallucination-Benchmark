# RESEARCH PROBLEM
## BACKGROUND
LLM's(Large language models) are used for complex and easy task which humans takes time to do like helping in programming,solving queries etc.
But the most of the LLM's hallucinate very easily that means that they do not perform welll in tasks given to them which can be harmful in some caases that are 
senstive. Hallucination simply means that giving wrong information or giving wrong answers for the question because of less data given or limitations of models.

## Problem Statement
Diffrent models hallucinate diffrently in each scenario making it harder to predict which model is right or wrong without fact check like a model can perform tasks 
well in programming but it can hallucinate in general knowledge. All the models don't hallucinate equally some do in maths ,some in science or some in other it 
depends on the question and model. One of the main factor is subject like programing ,maths, medical etc. Medcial is the subject where most of the models hallucinate
frequently and we are not advised to take any serious medical advice from LLM's or any other source. We can compare them fairly if we have and right approach on 
comparing them but it will be not be completely fair.

## Why this problem matters 
This problem is very important in most of the cases, for example : A person asked to solve a bug of an a program which is very important and the model Solved the bug
but deleted and important line of code now this can ruin a person's hardwork ,another example a person asked a model to recommend some medicine or treatment or viral
fever ,the model may hallucinate and give wrong medicine name or recommned a treatment which can worse the fever this can be a risk to life.That the reason that 
models hallucination can be very harmful specailly in real life based scenario.

## What this research paper will do 
This research aims to develop a reusable benchmark for comparing diffrent models hallucination rates by asking same question form diffrent subjects like: programming,
general knowledege, maths, medical etc. On predefined evaluation criteria and the research will be kept very fair for all models to get best output and results 
and somewaht help the community for further research and  rating an model.It will be open source after the comepletion of the research so the community can also 
contribute and correct the mistakes of the research paper.
