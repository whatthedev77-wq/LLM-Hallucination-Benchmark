## Hallucination Rate

Hallucination rate measures the propotion of evaulated responses classified as hallucinated.

Purpose:
To compare frequency of hallucinated responses between models and domains.

## Accuracy
Accuracy measures the proportion of responses classified as correct.

Purpose:
To determine whether lower hallucination rates are associated with higher overall correctness.


## Refusal Rate
Refusal Rate measures how often a model refuses or abstains from answering.
Purpose:
To distinguish a cautious model from a model that frequently generates unsupported answers.

## Partial Correctness Rate

This metric measures the proportion of responses classified as partially correct.

## Domain-Level Metrics
The rate will be calculated as per the different domains for each model.

## Difficulty-Level Metrics
The rate will be calculated in favour of difficulty level, For each level rate would be calulated.
