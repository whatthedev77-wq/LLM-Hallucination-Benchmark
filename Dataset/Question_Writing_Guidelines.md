## Question Sources

Questions will be created or selected from reliable and verifiable sources.

Every question must have a documented source or verification method.

Questions adopted or adapted from external datasets will follow their licensing and attribution requirements.

## Question Requirements

Every benchmark question must:

1. Have a clearly defined expected answer or evaluation criteria.
2. Be verifiable using reliable sources or deterministic methods.
3. Avoid unnecessary ambiguity.
4. Test the intended domain or capability.
5. Have documented metadata.
6. Be reviewed before inclusion.

## Questions to Avoid

The benchmark should avoid:

- Subjective opinion questions.
- Questions with multiple equally valid answers unless explicitly supported by the evaluation rubric.
- Poorly defined or ambiguous questions.
- Questions requiring hidden context.
- Questions with outdated information unless temporal knowledge is intentionally being tested.
- Questions where the ground truth cannot be reliably verified.

Design:
Idea for Question
       ↓
Write Draft Question
       ↓
Find / Create Ground Truth
       ↓
Verify with Reliable Source or Method
       ↓
Assign Domain and Metadata
       ↓
Review for Ambiguity
       ↓
Add to Candidate Dataset
       ↓
Pilot Test Later
       ↓
Final Benchmark Dataset
